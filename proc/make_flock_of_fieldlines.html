Build a flock of field lines spanning the region between neighbouring maxima contours centered around the minima contours through the field origin.

The field lines are placed equidistant in the label xi, spaced so that the discrete set still displays stellarator symmetry. To achieve this symmetry respecting spacing with < max_n_fieldlines, iota needs to be approximated. Its approximation is then used as rotational transform of the field lines. M_pol and N_tor are integer-valued reals giving the dominant helicity. N_tor must equal nfp when nonzero; use N_tor=0, M_pol=1 for QA. If the violation is too strong, maxima of contours of the omnigenous field are not merely deformed, but also split. Results should be treated with caution.
